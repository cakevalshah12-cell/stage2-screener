How stocks are picked
  • Stage 2 (weekly chart): close above a rising 30-week average, a weekly close above the prior 26-week high within the last 6 weeks, beating the Nifty 500 over 26 weeks, and at least ₹5 cr traded a day.
  • Just entering: the 30-week average was flat (within 5%) ten weeks ago.
  • Volume ×: breakout-week volume divided by the 10-week average. Two or more is a strong breakout.
  • Strong fundamentals: market cap over ₹1,000 cr, latest-quarter revenue growth 15%+ and profit growth 20%+, ROE 15%+ and debt/equity 0.75 or less (banks: ROA 1%+), and no more than 5% of promoter shares pledged.
  • Promoter 1y: change in promoter holding over a year, in percentage points. A big fall can be an OFS or QIP, not only promoters selling.
  • Prices from Upstox, growth and ROE from Yahoo Finance (one-off items can distort them), promoter holding and pledge from NSE filings. A shortlist to check by chart and results, not a buy list or investment advice.
